# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Cashiers, store managers, and retail associates operating primarily on mobile devices (and occasionally laptops) in fast-paced retail environments.

## Product Purpose
A high-density Point of Sale (POS) system designed to process transactions, manage inventory, and view reports rapidly with zero friction. Success means a cashier can complete an invoice accurately in under 5 seconds without misclicking or searching for essential controls.

## Brand Personality
Professional, Crisp, Utility-First, Confident.
Matches the high-end industrial exactness of Square POS or Shopify POS. 

## Anti-references
- Cluttered legacy ERPs or dated Windows POS systems.
- "AI Beige" or soft, washed-out color palettes.
- Overcrowded screens that dump all data simultaneously without hierarchy.
- Hidden or hard-to-reach primary actions (e.g., checkout buttons that aren't sticky).

## Design Principles
1. **Thumb-Driven Architecture**: Primary actions must live at the bottom of the screen (bottom nav, sticky checkout bars). Secondary actions and context live in a Top Bar.
2. **Progressive Disclosure**: Hide complexity by default (e.g., collapse product variants into a dropdown/accordion; only show them when a user actively explores the product).
3. **Absolute Legibility**: Razor-sharp high contrast, massive touch targets (minimum 44x44px), and clear hierarchical spacing for rapid visual parsing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High-contrast text readability is non-negotiable for retail lighting environments.
- Generous, fat-finger-friendly touch targets to reduce mis-taps during rushes.
- Native mobile keyboard support (e.g., `type="number"` for numeric input) so the OS handles entry naturally.
